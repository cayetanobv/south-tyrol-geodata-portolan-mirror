# Pharmacies — mirror of «Farmacie», Provincia Autonoma di Bolzano

Community mirror of the dataset «Farmacie» published by the Provincia Autonoma di Bolzano – Alto Adige / Autonome Provinz Bozen – Südtirol as open data (CC0 1.0). Extracted on 2026-09-03 from WFS layer p_bz-Health:Pharmacies and converted to GeoParquet. This copy is NOT the official publication: the authoritative source is the Province's geoportal and open-data portal (https://data.civis.bz.it/dataset/farmacie). Published for the OGC Metadata Summit 2026 demo.
