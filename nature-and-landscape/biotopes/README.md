# Biotopes — mirror of «Biotopi», Provincia Autonoma di Bolzano

Community mirror of the dataset «Biotopi» published by the Provincia Autonoma di Bolzano – Alto Adige / Autonome Provinz Bozen – Südtirol as open data (CC0 1.0). Extracted on 2026-09-03 from WFS layer p_bz-TerritorialPlans:LandscapePlan-Biotopes and converted to GeoParquet. This copy is NOT the official publication: the authoritative source is the Province's geoportal and open-data portal (https://data.civis.bz.it/dataset/biotopi).
